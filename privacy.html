Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

Flash Dox (“the app”) is a document scanner published by SWATNOA (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app.

In short: Flash Dox works entirely on your phone. We don’t collect, store or share your personal data. Your scans never leave your device unless you share or save them yourself.

Your documents stay on your device

Photos you take or import, the documents, PDFs and images you create, text recognised from your scans, your saved signature and your settings are kept only in the app’s private storage on your device. We have no servers that receive them, and we cannot see them.

They leave your device only when you choose to: for example when you tap Share (which opens your phone’s share sheet and sends the file to the app you pick), or Save to phone (which saves a copy to your gallery or a folder you choose). Once shared, the file is handled under the privacy policy of whichever app or service you sent it to.

No internet access on Android

The Android version of Flash Dox does not request the internet permission, so it cannot send anything over the network. The only time a web page opens is when you tap a link such as this policy or the FAQ, which opens in your browser.

On-device features

Some features use Google ML Kit libraries that are built into the app and run on your device:

  • Extract text reads text from your scans.
  • Passport photo backgrounds find the outline of the person so the background can be changed to white or blue.

Your images and the text found in them are processed on your device and are not sent to Google or to us. On iPhone and iPad, where apps cannot be denied network access, ML Kit may send Google anonymous technical information (such as device model, operating system version, app version and performance metrics) to maintain the service; it never includes your images or text. See Google’s ML Kit data disclosure and ML Kit terms.

Permissions

CameraTo scan documents, ID cards and passports, and to take passport photos. Used only while you are on a scan screen. No audio or video is recorded.

Flash Dox does not ask for your location, microphone, contacts, calendar, or account details.

What we don’t do

  • No account or sign-in.
  • No advertising and no advertising ID.
  • No analytics, tracking or crash reporting services.
  • We don’t sell, rent or share personal data with anyone.

If a future version adds anything that changes this, such as ads or crash reports, we will update this policy before that version is released.

Keeping and deleting your data

Everything stays on your device until you delete it. Deleting a page or document in the app removes it from the app’s storage, and uninstalling the app removes all of its data. Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

Your scans are not backed up by us. To keep a copy, share or save it before you uninstall the app or change phones.

Security

Your documents are kept in storage that only Flash Dox can read, protected by your device’s own security (such as its screen lock and encryption). Anyone who can unlock your phone and open the app can see your scans, so keep your device locked.

Children

Flash Dox is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU and UK, or the CCPA in California), you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data a company holds about you. We don’t hold any personal data about our users, but you’re welcome to contact us with any question or request.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will post the new version on this page and update the “Last updated” date above.
