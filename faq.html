The basics

Is Flash Dox free?

Yes. Every feature is free, there are no ads, and you don’t need an account.

Does it work without internet?

Yes, completely. Scanning, filters, PDF making, text extraction, signing and passport photos all run on your phone. The Android app doesn’t even have internet access.

Where are my scans stored? Are they uploaded anywhere?

They’re stored only inside Flash Dox on your phone and are never uploaded. They leave your phone only when you share them or save them to your gallery or files. See the Privacy Policy.

If I change phones or reinstall, will my documents come back?

No. Flash Dox has no cloud backup, so uninstalling the app deletes your scans. Before you uninstall or switch phones, open each document you want to keep and use Share or Save to phone to keep a copy.

Scanning

How do I scan a document with several pages?

Choose Docs, hold the phone flat over the first page and take the photo. Keep going for each page, then tap Done. You can reorder, add or delete pages afterwards.

The edges of my page were detected wrongly. How do I fix it?

Open the page, tap Edit, and drag the corner handles onto the corners of the paper. For best results, put the page on a darker surface than the paper, use even light, and avoid shadows.

What do the filters do?
  • Auto: cleans up the page and evens out lighting. Good for most documents.
  • Color boost: brighter, punchier colours.
  • Grayscale and B&W print: smaller files, ideal for text you’ll print.
  • ID colour: tuned for cards and passports, keeps photos and holograms natural.
  • Original: no changes.

You can pick a default filter in Settings.

Can I scan photos I already have?

Yes. Tap Import and choose from your gallery or files. On Android you can also share images to Flash Dox from another app.

The app says it needs camera access.

Flash Dox only uses the camera while you’re scanning. If you said no earlier, tap Open settings on that screen, or go to your phone’s Settings → Apps → Flash Dox → Permissions and allow the camera. You can also import photos instead.

ID cards and passports

How do I get both sides of my ID card on one page?

Choose ID Card and scan the front, then the back. Open the card and tap Print. Pick PDF one page for both sides on a single A4 sheet, or PDF double-sided so each back prints behind its front.

My printed card came out the wrong size.

In your printer settings, choose 100% or Actual size, not “Fit to page” or “Shrink to fit”. Cards are laid out at their real size (85.6 × 54 mm), so scaling makes them too small. For double-sided printing, choose flip on long edge.

Passport photos

Which passport photo sizes are supported?

35 × 45 mm (used in the UK, EU, Pakistan, India and many other countries), 2 × 2 in (US), or a custom size in millimetres. Always check your country’s official photo rules for size, head position and background colour.

Can I change the background to white or blue?

Yes. On the crop screen choose White or Dark blue. The app finds you in the photo and replaces the background on your phone. It works best with a plain wall behind you, even lighting, and hair that stands out from the wall. If the edges don’t look right, try another photo or keep Original.

How do I print passport photos?

After cropping, choose Print sheet. Pick A4 or 4 × 6 in photo paper and how many photos you want (4, 8, 12 or 16), then save or share the PDF. Print it at 100% / Actual size. Photo shops can print the 4 × 6 sheet on glossy photo paper.

A website wants one photo with an exact size or a file under 100 KB.

After cropping, choose Single photo. Pick JPG or PNG, then use Image size to set the exact pixels the form asks for (for example 600 × 771) and Max file size to keep it under 200 KB, 100 KB or 50 KB. Then tap Save to phone or Share.

Sharing and file size

Which formats can I export?

PDF (one file with all pages), JPG and PNG (one image per page). Passport photos export as JPG or PNG. Choose A4, Letter or Fit to image for PDF page size.

My file is too big to upload or email. How do I make it smaller?

When sharing, tap Max file size and pick a limit such as 1 MB, 500 KB or 200 KB, or set your own. Flash Dox shrinks the file to fit and tells you the final size. The Shrink PDF tool on the home screen does the same for any document in Flash Dox. Standard quality also makes smaller files than High.

Tools

How do I copy text from a scan?

Open the document and use Extract text. You can copy it, edit it first, or share it as a .txt file. Text recognition runs on your phone and works best with printed text in Latin-script languages such as English, French, Spanish or German.

How do I sign a document?

Use Sign document. Draw your signature with your finger or take a photo of it signed on white paper; it’s saved only on your phone for next time. Drag it into place, resize it from the corner, and add the date if you need it.

Can I recover a document I deleted?

No. Deleting is permanent, which is why the app asks you to confirm first.
